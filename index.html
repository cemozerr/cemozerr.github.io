About

I build blockchain infrastructure. I co-founded Sovereign Labs in 2021 and was its CEO until Celestia Labs acquired the company in July 2026. I now lead strategy at Celestia Labs as Chief Strategy Officer.

Before Sovereign, I was an Ethereum core developer at ConsenSys, working on Teku, the open-source Ethereum consensus client written in Java. I also co-founded Plaintext Capital, a long-only, momentum-based crypto hedge fund.
